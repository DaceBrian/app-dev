# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is one of my favorite series because it mixes science fiction, mystery, and emotional storytelling.

## Why I Love This Series
- Great character development  
- Exciting and mysterious plot  
- 80s aesthetic and soundtrack  
- Strong friendships and emotional moments  

## Main Characters
1. **Eleven**
2. **Mike Wheeler**
3. **Dustin Henderson**
4. **Lucas Sinclair**
5. **Will Byers**

> *"Friends don't lie." — Eleven*

### Final Thoughts
I enjoy how the series balances supernatural elements with real human experiences. It’s definitely a must-watch!
